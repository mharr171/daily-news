General news

About $164 million has flowed through Mayor Lurie’s political and charity network

The San Francisco Standard tallied roughly $124 million in private donations to city efforts and $39 million in political money tied to Lurie since 2023, plus $10 million of his own in 2024. Four November measures he backs, including three charter reforms and a Muni parcel tax, have raised $18.6 million.

Why it matters: Many Peninsula commuters rely on Muni and BART connections in the city, so the San Francisco measures on this ballot shape regional transit too.

The San Francisco Standard, Oct 28, 2026

Most California Republicans in Congress skip a pledge to seat election winners

A bipartisan pledge to oppose efforts to undermine free and fair elections has 17 signers, including five California Democrats. Rep. Kevin Kiley, now an independent, was the only member of California’s Republican delegation to sign; several others did not respond. The pledge aims to head off a repeat of the pressure on Congress after the 2020 election.

Why it matters: With the House majority razor-thin, how California’s representatives handle certification could matter for the whole country.

CalMatters, Oct 27, 2026

Astronomers spot a possible new dwarf planet beyond Neptune

Data from the Vera C. Rubin Observatory revealed 2026 ED138, an object estimated at 600 to 1,500 kilometers across about 46 times farther from the sun than Earth. If its size is confirmed, likely with infrared telescopes such as JWST, it would become the sixth known dwarf planet.
