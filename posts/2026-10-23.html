Issue No. 23

Friday, October 23, 2026

San Mateo County voters will decide whether supervisors get more time to fill vacant elected offices, and San Francisco health officials report the city’s first death from a potent kratom-derived opioid sold in gas stations. The Warriors play their home opener tonight as the 49ers prepare for Atlanta with a depleted receiving corps, and NASA briefs the public today on plans to speed up its return to the moon.

In this edition:

General news

San Francisco reports its first death from a gas-station opioid called MGM-15

San Francisco’s health department issued an alert Wednesday after a resident died in late September from MGM-15, a kratom-derived opioid estimated to be up to 50 times stronger than morphine. The tablets, sold at gas stations and smoke shops, did not list the drug on the package. Officials urge anyone who sees a possible overdose to give naloxone and call 911.

Why it matters: The same products are sold in convenience stores across the Peninsula, and mislabeling means buyers may not know what they are taking.

CBS News Bay Area, Oct 22, 2026

Measure J would give San Mateo County supervisors 60 days to fill vacancies

Measure J, a county charter amendment on the Nov. 3 ballot, would double the time supervisors have to appoint someone or call a special election for a vacant elected office, from 30 to 60 days. It would also require at least one public meeting before an appointment. Supervisors placed it on the ballot after the rushed decision that followed the removal of former Sheriff Christina Corpus; no opposing argument was filed.

Why it matters: Every San Mateo County voter, including in Millbrae, will see Measure J, and it affects how future vacancies in county offices are filled.

The Almanac, Oct 22, 2026

US pulls nonessential embassy staff from Saudi Arabia over Houthi attacks

The State Department ordered nonemergency U.S. government employees and their families to leave Saudi Arabia after Iranian-backed Houthi forces stepped up attacks on Saudi airports, oil sites, and shipping. A new security alert warns Americans of possible airport attacks and travel disruptions. Britain said it will send fighter jets to help defend the kingdom.

Why it matters: Fighting around the Gulf can push up oil prices and disrupt travel for Bay Area residents with family or business in the region.

CNN (via KESQ), Oct 22, 2026

Science & tech

NASA holds its “Artemis acceleration” briefing today

NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman and Artemis leaders will speak at 11 a.m. Pacific from Kennedy Space Center about speeding up the moon program. The update covers Artemis III, a 2027 crewed test of docking with lunar landers in Earth orbit, and the push to land astronauts in 2028. The briefing streams live online.

Space.com, Oct 22, 2026

Anthropic previews Claude Duet, an agent that works in the background

Anthropic’s new Claude Duet app can build daily briefings and dashboards and run tasks on its own cloud computer, checking back with users when it needs approval. It can make purchases through Stripe Link or Shop Pay, with each payment needing a user’s OK, and declines requests for card or ID numbers by default. The research preview is open by waitlist to Pro and Max subscribers on iOS, Android, and the web.

TechCrunch, Oct 22, 2026

Europe’s Space Rider crashes in a parachute drop test

A full-scale mockup of ESA’s reusable Space Rider vehicle crashed during an Oct. 6 test over Sardinia when its main parafoil detached after release from a helicopter. An independent investigation is under way, and ESA has recovered all the parts. The agency says the setback is not on the critical path and still targets a first orbital mission later this decade.

Space.com, Oct 22, 2026
