Issue No. 14

Wednesday, October 14, 2026

PG&E is warning about 70,000 customers in 29 counties of wildfire-safety shutoffs that could begin this evening, and the Justice Department is threatening California with legal action over voter citizenship three weeks before Election Day. The Warriors rallied past the Lakers in Las Vegas, and Dre Greenlaw lost his suspension appeal. Google launched Gemini 4 Argon, and NASA’s Perseverance rover set a new off-world driving record.

In this edition:

General news

PG&E warns about 70,000 customers of power shutoffs starting Wednesday evening

PG&E told roughly 70,000 customers in 29 counties, including more than 4,700 in Alameda County and 1,600 in Contra Costa County, that it may cut power starting around 5:30 p.m. Wednesday because strong winds could bring down lines and spark wildfires. The outages could last until Saturday afternoon, and the areas affected may shift with the forecast.

Why it matters: San Mateo County isn’t named in the warning, but the list can change with the weather, so Peninsula residents should check their address with PG&E.

NBC Bay Area, Oct 13, 2026

Justice Department gives California 48 hours to verify voter citizenship

The Justice Department gave California 48 hours to verify the citizenship of voters it has flagged or face federal legal action, according to a letter reported by Reuters. The demand comes three weeks before the Nov. 3 election and follows July letters warning election officials in all 50 states that they could be prosecuted if they knowingly let noncitizens stay on the rolls or vote.

Why it matters: San Mateo County voters can check their own ballot’s status with the state’s free Where’s My Ballot service while the dispute plays out.

Reuters (TimesLIVE), Oct 14, 2026

Bipartisan push for kids’ online safety rules grows as Meta lobbies for its own approach

Lawmakers from both parties, state attorneys general, school districts and public health groups are pressing for limits on social media companies, and Congress is edging closer to the Kids Online Safety Act, an earlier version of which passed the Senate with 91 votes. Meta opposes blanket bans for kids and has spent $10 million on ads for its teen accounts, while the ACLU warns that age checks could threaten speech and privacy.

Why it matters: New federal rules would shape what Bay Area kids see online and would fall heavily on Menlo Park-based Meta.

KFF Health News, Oct 13, 2026

Pope Leo diagnosed with lung cancer after surgery

The Vatican said Pope Leo XIV, 71, has lung adenocarcinoma, confirmed during a roughly 90-minute operation at Rome’s Gemelli hospital that removed the lower lobe of his right lung. Doctors called the surgery successful and the prognosis excellent because the cancer was caught early, and his audiences are canceled through Oct. 20.

Why it matters: The health of the first American pope is closely followed by the Bay Area’s large Catholic community, including parishes across San Mateo County.

Associated Press (MPR News), Oct 13, 2026

Russian strikes leave 600,000 Ukrainians without power despite a declared energy truce

Russia fired about 39 ballistic missiles and more than 120 drones at Ukraine overnight, leaving at least 600,000 people without power early Wednesday and killing at least one person in Kyiv, the city’s mayor said. President Trump had announced an energy ceasefire on Sunday, and Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said the strikes deliberately targeted energy sites covered by it.

Why it matters: Attacks on energy infrastructure keep global fuel markets jittery, which feeds into the gas prices Californians pay.

ABC News, Oct 14, 2026

Sports

Today in sports: Warriors rally past the Lakers in Las Vegas; Quakes visit Salt Lake tonight

Golden State erased a 13-point halftime deficit to beat the Lakers 101–89 in Las Vegas on Tuesday, holding Los Angeles to 13 points in each of the last two quarters and improving to 3–2 in the preseason. Yaxel Lendeborg and Brandin Podziemski scored 17 points each and Steph Curry added 11 in 22 minutes, though Lendeborg sat out the fourth quarter after appearing to twist his left ankle. The Earthquakes play at Real Salt Lake tonight, and the 49ers host Washington on Monday night.

NBC Sports Bay Area, Oct 13, 2026

Greenlaw loses his appeal and will miss Monday night’s game against Washington

A hearing officer appointed jointly by the NFL and the players’ union upheld linebacker Dre Greenlaw’s one-game suspension for headbutting Seattle’s Ernest Jones IV during Sunday’s 32–29 win. Greenlaw, the 49ers’ second-leading tackler with 37 stops and a forced fumble, will sit out the Week 6 Monday night game against the Commanders at Levi’s Stadium. He can rejoin the active roster the next day.

NBC Sports Bay Area, Oct 13, 2026

Culture

“Resident Evil” becomes the franchise’s biggest film worldwide

Zach Cregger’s Resident Evil reboot from Sony has earned about $323 million worldwide, the most of the series’ eight films, including roughly $141 million in North America since its Sept. 18 opening. It added an estimated $62.6 million globally last weekend, most of it overseas, where it also topped China’s box office.

FilmoFilia, Oct 14, 2026

“Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2” trailer previews a new Night City story ahead of its Netflix debut

Studio Trigger’s anime sequel tells a standalone story with new characters, including a veteran mercenary haunted by his reputation, a netrunner hunting his clan’s killer and a young filmmaker documenting real lives. Kai Ikarashi directs, and the series arrives on Netflix on Tuesday, Oct. 20.

FilmoFilia, Oct 14, 2026

“Blue Eye Samurai” adds Jonathan Pryce, Joel Kim Booster and Sherry Cola for season 2

The new voice cast was announced at IGN Fan Fest: Pryce as Old Violet, Booster as Priest Ando and Cola as Kuma. The animated Netflix series’ second season is due in January 2027, with a third and final season planned for 2028.

FilmoFilia, Oct 14, 2026

Science & tech

Google launches Gemini 4 Argon for Pro and Ultra subscribers

Google’s newest frontier model is available now to Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers in the Gemini app and in AI Mode in Search, and to developers at an introductory $2 per million input tokens through Nov. 30. Google says Argon is built for long, multi-step work like coding and financial or legal analysis, writes with less hedging, and better resists prompt-injection attacks. GitHub also added Argon to Copilot for Pro+, Max, Business and Enterprise users.

Google, Oct 13, 2026

NASA finishes stacking the Artemis III boosters at Kennedy

Teams at Kennedy Space Center completed the solid rocket booster segments and nose cones for Artemis III, and all four RS-25 engines are now installed on the core stage. In the coming weeks the core stage will be joined to the boosters, and NASA then plans to roll the partial stack to Launch Pad 39B for a fueling test while the four-person crew rehearses launch day.

NASA, Oct 13, 2026

Perseverance breaks Opportunity’s record for the longest drive on another world

NASA’s Perseverance rover passed 28.10 miles on Mars on Oct. 11, edging out the 28.06-mile record that Opportunity took about 14 years to set; Perseverance did it in about five and a half. The rover is heading south along the rim of Jezero Crater toward an area that may hold clay minerals formed by water, a key target in the search for signs of past life.

Space.com, Oct 13, 2026
