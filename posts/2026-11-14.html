Top story

SFO digs out after Friday’s nationwide ground stop, with delays likely into the weekend

The FAA halted departures at every U.S. airport at 12:54 p.m. Friday after a communications failure at air traffic control centers, stopping flights at SFO, Oakland and San José. Some Pacific-bound flights left SFO by mid-afternoon and the stop was lifted Friday evening, but about 5,000 U.S. flights were canceled; Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy blamed aging equipment and warned the recovery would be slow, and SFO says delays could last into the weekend. Travelers whose flights were canceled or delayed three hours or more (six for international trips) can claim a full cash refund if they decline rebooking and vouchers.

Why it matters: If you fly out of SFO this weekend, check your flight status before you leave home and expect crowded rebooking lines.

The San Francisco Standard, Nov 13, 2026

Developing

Mavericks could see 50-foot waves today as a high surf warning covers the coast

After Friday’s storm, the National Weather Service’s high surf warning continues through Saturday, with breakers of 20 to 30 feet along the coast and possibly 50 feet at favored spots such as Mavericks off Half Moon Bay. Santa Cruz has closed all city beaches and its wharf until 5 a.m. Sunday, and Marin County says it has handed out more than 30,000 sandbags since October. Forecasters call this the first of several storms expected over the coming week.

Why it matters: Stay off rocks, jetties and beaches on the San Mateo coast today; sneaker waves can sweep people far up the sand.

San Mateo County court workers plan to strike Monday

Superior Court employees represented by SEIU Local 521 say they will walk out Monday, accusing court management of bargaining in bad faith; 99% of members authorized the strike. The court says it has offered 3% raises plus a one-time payment, met with the union again Friday, and will keep every courthouse open, though some cases may be delayed. Arraignments, custody matters and emergency domestic violence restraining orders will go ahead.

Why it matters: If you have a court date or filing in San Mateo County on Monday, check the court’s website that morning for changes.

Good news

Everyone aboard is accounted for after a tanker capsizes a riverboat in New Orleans

A tanker that appeared to lose steering slammed into the docked paddle wheeler American Heritage on the Mississippi River on Friday, rolling it over and throwing people into the water. Harbor crews, firefighters and bystanders on nearby boats pulled people out, and New Orleans’ mayor said everyone was accounted for, calling it “a miracle on the Mississippi.” Thirty-two people went to hospitals, none with critical injuries, and the Coast Guard is investigating.

U.S. politics & government

Trump will meet Xi and Putin together in China on Thursday, a first for the three leaders

The White House says President Trump, China’s Xi Jinping and Russia’s Vladimir Putin will hold their first three-way meeting in Shenzhen on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific summit, where Trump wants to talk about cutting nuclear arsenals and is expected to raise the Iran war. Officials say Ukraine, which is on a separate negotiating track, isn’t on the agenda, and export controls won’t be negotiated. Trump leaves Monday, stops at the ASEAN summit in the Philippines on Tuesday, and also has a separate meeting with Xi planned.

Why it matters: Talks with China shape tariffs and trade that run through California’s ports and tech companies.

RTÉ News (AFP), Nov 14, 2026

Earthquakes and PayPal Park are being sold for $625 million

A’s owner John Fisher has agreed to sell the Earthquakes and their stadium to Heath Freeman, president and co-founder of the investment firm Alden Global Capital, ending an ownership that began in 2007. Fisher put the club up for sale in June 2025 while he finances the A’s ballpark in Las Vegas. The deal comes as the Quakes, the West’s No. 4 seed after their best season in years, prepare for their first playoff match at PayPal Park on Nov. 24.

Deebo Samuel returns from a hamstring injury and newly acquired Keenan Allen is set to debut, while cornerbacks Deommodore Lenoir and Renardo Green were limited Friday.

Developing

Starship reaches orbit, but its booster is lost; SpaceX tries to catch the ship today

Starship Flight 15 lifted off from Texas Friday afternoon, but the Super Heavy booster called off its return to the launch tower about six minutes in after detecting a guidance problem, diverted to the Gulf of Mexico and exploded just before hitting the water. The upper stage reached orbit, released 26 Starlink satellites, and is due to fly back to Starbase on Saturday after about 14 orbits for the first attempt to catch a ship with the tower’s arms. SpaceX can still choose to end the flight in the ocean instead.
