Issue No. 25

Sunday, October 25, 2026

Seven more San Mateo County vote centers are open with Election Day nine days away, as voters weigh Measure U and about $44 billion in state bonds. The unbeaten 49ers visit Atlanta today after the Warriors fell to 0–3, and October’s full Hunter’s Moon rises tonight.

In this edition:

General news

Seven more San Mateo County vote centers open, bringing the total to 10

New vote centers opened Saturday in Burlingame, Daly City, East Palo Alto, Half Moon Bay, Pacifica, San Carlos and a second Redwood City site, joining three that opened Oct. 5. They are open daily from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. through Nov. 2 and from 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. on Election Day, Nov. 3. Voters can cast a ballot, get a replacement, use accessible machines, or register and vote the same day.

Why it matters: The Burlingame site is the closest new option for Millbrae voters who want to vote in person before Nov. 3.

San Mateo County Elections, Oct 23, 2026

California voters weigh about $44 billion in housing and research bonds

Three measures on the Nov. 3 ballot would put about $44 billion toward housing and science. Prop. 1 is an $11.25 billion affordable housing bond, Prop. 37 offers down payment help on new homes funded by private lenders, and Prop. 38 would commit $8.4 billion to immunology research, half of it to a single institute. The state already spends about $6 billion a year repaying past bonds.

Why it matters: Prop. 37’s price cap for eligible homes is about $1.5 million in the Bay Area, and bond repayments come out of the state budget every year.

CalMatters, Oct 24, 2026

San Mateo County’s Measure U would take district map-drawing away from supervisors

Measure U would create an independent commission to draw the county’s five supervisorial districts, a job the Board of Supervisors now controls. Supporters, including Supervisors Jackie Speier and Lisa Gauthier and the League of Women Voters, point to 2021, when the board set aside its citizen advisers’ maps. No opposing argument was filed.

Why it matters: District lines decide which supervisor represents Millbrae and how Peninsula neighborhoods are grouped for the next decade.

Redwood City Pulse, Oct 24, 2026

US pulls some diplomats from Middle East posts over Iranian threats

The State Department ordered non-emergency staff and all family members out of Saudi Arabia and quietly reduced staffing in Jordan, Lebanon, Qatar and the UAE. An Oct. 23 memo to Secretary of State Marco Rubio says Iran has stepped up efforts to target US personnel, while Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia add to the risk. Many diplomats had only recently returned after earlier evacuations.

Why it matters: Renewed tension in the region can push up fuel prices and affect travel plans for Bay Area residents with family there.

CNN via KESQ, Oct 24, 2026

Science & tech

Hunter’s Moon reaches full tonight

October’s full moon, the Hunter’s Moon, hits 100% illumination at 9:12 p.m. Pacific tonight after rising in the east around sunset. Watch soon after moonrise, when it can look larger and orange near the horizon. A full moon is also a good time to pick out the dark lava plains and bright crater rays with binoculars.

Space.com, Oct 25, 2026

Giant black holes can’t explain dark matter, physicists find

Physicists in Germany and Abu Dhabi checked whether black holes more than 100 billion times the Sun’s mass could make up much of the universe’s dark matter. The cosmic microwave background shows none of the clumping such objects would cause, so they can’t be a major share. The study is due in Physical Review Letters on Monday, and the team is now testing much smaller black holes.
