General news

Halloween weekend brings a coastal flood advisory and a chance of drizzle

A coastal flood advisory covers shoreline areas of San Francisco and San Pablo bays through 6 p.m. Sunday, with up to 1.9 feet of water possible in low spots. Rain spreads into the Bay Area later Saturday, so trick-or-treaters may see light drizzle, with highs in the low 60s.

Why it matters: Plan Halloween routes away from flood-prone bayside streets near high tide, and bring a light rain layer for kids.

KPIX via KION, Oct 30, 2026

Could Prop. 45 open the door to more California data centers?

Prop. 45 is pitched as a pro-housing overhaul of environmental review, but legal scholars say its streamlining for telecom and support infrastructure could let data centers sidestep a new state law. Supporters, who have raised more than $40 million including $3 million from PG&E, say data centers aren’t covered, and at least 26 cities and counties have moved to limit them.

Why it matters: Data centers draw heavily on the power grid and water, which affects utility bills and local planning on the Peninsula.

Astronauts mark the 100th US spacewalk from the space station

NASA’s Anil Menon and Canada’s Joshua Kutryk spent 7 hours and 11 minutes outside the station installing an upgraded solar array that unrolled to 63 feet on its own. It was the first spacewalk by a Canadian since 2019, and the pair go out again Nov. 5 to add the last new array.

Virgin Galactic’s new space plane flies for the first time

Apeiron, the first of Virgin Galactic’s Delta-class ships, flew beneath its carrier plane from Arizona to Spaceport America in New Mexico for testing. The company plans to start carrying paying customers in spring 2027, with nearly 700 ticket holders waiting.
