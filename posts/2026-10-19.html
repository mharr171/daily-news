Issue No. 19

Monday, October 19, 2026

Today is the last day to register to vote online for the Nov. 3 election, and voters still missing a mail ballot have options. The 5-0 49ers host Washington tonight after a weekend in which the Valkyries won Game 1 of the WNBA Finals and the Earthquakes clinched a playoff spot. Street Fighter won the box office with $30.8 million.

In this edition:

General news

Today is the last day to register to vote online for Nov. 3

Eligible Californians can register or update their registration online at registertovote.ca.gov until midnight tonight for the Nov. 3 general election. After that, they can still use same-day conditional registration in person at their county elections office or any vote center through Election Day; those ballots are counted once the county verifies them. Voters can check their status at voterstatus.sos.ca.gov.

Why it matters: San Mateo County residents who have moved or never registered can still do it from home today and skip a trip to a vote center later.

Redwood City Pulse, Oct 19, 2026

What to do if your mail ballot hasn’t arrived

Bay Area counties had to begin mailing ballots by Oct. 5, so voters still waiting should check the state’s voter status page or the Where’s My Ballot tool. Voters who moved should re-register at their new address, and others can request a replacement from their county, though counties stop mailing replacements six days before Election Day and after that issue them in person. More early voting locations open starting Oct. 24 and again Oct. 31.

Why it matters: With some San Mateo County ballots stolen from a Daly City post office last week, checking your ballot’s status is a quick safeguard.

KQED, Oct 19, 2026

No Kings protests turn into an early-voting push

Thousands turned out Saturday for No Kings Vote Early events in cities from Washington, D.C., to Minneapolis, as organizers focused on turnout, early voting and training poll watchers. The coalition, which includes Indivisible, the ACLU and Black Voters Matter, called the effort nonpartisan and barred endorsements and current candidates from speaking.

Why it matters: With San Mateo County ballots already in mailboxes, groups across the spectrum are focused on getting voters to return them early.

Sports

Weekend recap: Valkyries strike first, Quakes clinch, 49ers play tonight

Friday: the Warriors hosted Portland in a preseason game at Chase Center. Saturday: the Valkyries beat the Atlanta Dream 68-64 in Game 1 of the WNBA Finals, and the Earthquakes tied Nashville 1-1 to clinch their first playoff spot since 2023. This week: the 49ers (5-0) host Washington tonight at 5:15, the Warriors open Wednesday at the Lakers and host Memphis on Friday, the Quakes visit FC Dallas on Saturday and the 49ers play at Atlanta Sunday at 10 a.m.

San Jose Earthquakes, Oct 17, 2026

49ers put their perfect start on the line in their first prime-time game

The unbeaten 49ers host the Washington Commanders tonight at Levi’s Stadium in their first prime-time game of the season, on ESPN and ABC at 5:15 p.m., during the team’s Alumni Weekend. They will be without Nick Bosa and Deebo Samuel, with Mike Evans and Christian Kirk questionable. Punter Corliss Waitman was named NFC Special Teams Player of the Week for his work in the win at Seattle.

San Francisco 49ers, Oct 17, 2026

Culture

Street Fighter wins the weekend with a $30.8 million opening

Street Fighter led the weekend with about $30.8 million from 3,575 theaters. You Can See Everything was second with $15.6 million, holdover Other Mommy took third with $8.2 million, and newcomers Whalefall ($7.8 million) and Sense and Sensibility ($7.2 million) rounded out the top five.

The Numbers, Oct 18, 2026

You Can See Everything posts the weekend’s best per-theater average

Playing in just 855 theaters, You Can See Everything averaged about $18,247 per location, more than double Street Fighter’s roughly $8,600. The Indian sequel Jailer 2 also opened in the top 10 with $3.1 million from 800 screens, and the top 20 films together grossed about $100.9 million.

Box Office Watch, Oct 18, 2026

Science & tech

SpaceX readies Bandwagon-5 rideshare and Starfall capsule for Tuesday launch

SpaceX is targeting 7:04 a.m. EDT (4:04 a.m. PDT) Tuesday for a Falcon 9 launch from Cape Canaveral carrying 31 customer payloads on its Bandwagon-5 rideshare mission. The flight also carries the company’s uncrewed Starfall reentry capsule on its second trip to space.
