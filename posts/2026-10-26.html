Issue No. 26

Monday, October 26, 2026

A five-county transit sales tax on the Nov. 3 ballot could decide whether Caltrain keeps weekend service, and a hit-and-run driver shut down Muni’s Market Street subway for part of the weekend. The 49ers’ unbeaten start ended in Atlanta, the Warriors are still looking for their first win, and SpaceX test-fired the booster for its next Starship flight.

In this edition:

General news

Regional transit tax would keep Caltrain and BART from deep cuts

Prop. RTM would add a half-cent sales tax in San Mateo, Alameda, Contra Costa and Santa Clara counties, and a full cent in San Francisco, for 14 years, raising nearly $1 billion a year for transit. Without it, Caltrain says it would drop weekend service, run hourly trains and stop at 9 p.m., while BART would close stations at 9 p.m. and cut to three lines. San Francisco voters also face Prop. H, a parcel tax for Muni.

Why it matters: Millbrae’s BART and Caltrain station would see far fewer trains if the measure fails, and county shoppers would pay the higher sales tax if it passes.

San Francisco Examiner, Oct 25, 2026

Car abandoned in Muni’s Market Street tunnel halts subway service

A driver steered a car into the Muni Metro tunnel near Folsom Street and the Embarcadero on Saturday afternoon, then fled on foot. Police are treating it as a hit-and-run; the car was towed after damaging city property. Subway service through the tunnel was disrupted Saturday evening and resumed Sunday morning.

Why it matters: Peninsula riders who transfer from BART or Caltrain to Muni downtown lost the subway for an evening.

Mission Local, Oct 25, 2026

San Mateo County waives late fees for unlicensed dogs and cats through November

Pet owners who have fallen behind on licensing can catch up without late fees or penalties until Nov. 30; regular license fees still apply. Dogs and cats need a license by three months of age or within 60 days of adoption or moving to the county. Licenses can be bought online through PetData, by mail, or in person with a current rabies certificate.

Why it matters: Millbrae residents can avoid penalties by licensing pets before the waiver ends Nov. 30.

San Mateo County News, Oct 24, 2026

Science & tech

SpaceX test-fires the Super Heavy booster for Starship Flight 15

SpaceX fired all 33 Raptor 3 engines on Super Heavy Booster 22 for about 15 seconds at Starbase in South Texas on Sunday. The booster will be paired with Ship 42 for Starship’s 15th test flight. SpaceX hasn’t set a date, but recent campaigns suggest liftoff could come in mid-to-late November.

Space.com, Oct 26, 2026

Skywatching this week: the ‘Demon Star’ dims and Taurid fireballs begin

The variable star Algol, in the southeast after sunset, fades noticeably around its eclipses on Thursday and next Sunday, though not on Halloween night. The Taurid meteor shower is active now and can produce rare, bright fireballs ahead of its early-November peak. The Pleiades climb high in the east before midnight.
