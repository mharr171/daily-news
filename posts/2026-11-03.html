Election Day in Millbrae: where to vote and what’s on the ballot

Polls are open from 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. today. Millbrae’s vote center is the Chetcuti Community Room at 450 Poplar Ave., and a ballot drop box outside City Hall at 621 Magnolia Ave. takes ballots until 8 p.m. Millbrae voters pick City Council members in three districts: District 2 (one candidate), District 4 (two candidates) and a partial term in District 3 (two candidates). Mail ballots must be postmarked by today and arrive within seven days.

Why it matters: Council races in Millbrae are often decided by small margins, and the county’s first results arrive tonight but the count continues for days.

Gas and grocery watch

Gas and grocery watch: California gas falls to $5.91, still $1.25 above a year ago

AAA’s California average for regular was $5.906 on Monday, down from $6.019 a week ago and $6.401 a month ago, but well above $4.651 a year ago; the national average is $4.169. San Mateo County isn’t listed separately by AAA, and the San Francisco metro area averaged $5.930. For groceries, the September consumer price report found food at home down 0.1% from August and up 1.8% over the year, while restaurant prices were up 3.5% over the year. Gasoline nationwide was up 34.2% over the year, and the October report comes out Nov. 10.

Why it matters: Cheaper gas helps, but a fill-up in the Bay Area still costs far more than a year ago, while store prices are rising more slowly than restaurant menus.

Good news

Tiny solar-powered trackers are revealing how monarch butterflies make their 3,000-mile trip

Scientists with the National Wildlife Federation and partners have attached radio tags, each about as heavy as a drop of water, to nearly 1,750 monarchs this year. About 175 tagged butterflies have been detected in central Mexico so far, including one that started west of the Rockies, and the data should show where monarchs rest and which habitats most need protection. The eastern population has fallen more than 80% since the mid-1990s and the western population about 95%.

Associated Press (via ABC News), Nov 3, 2026

U.S. politics & government

Top story

Americans vote in midterms that could end Republican control of Congress

All 435 House seats, 35 Senate seats and 36 governorships are on the ballot. Democrats need three House gains and a net four Senate seats to take both chambers; Republicans now hold the Senate 53–47. Decision Desk HQ’s final forecast shows Democrats winning 241 House seats and a 52–48 Senate majority, though polls can miss, and more than 57 million people voted early. Trump has said that if he loses Congress he could veto everything, though he said he might eventually strike deals.

Why it matters: California’s House races and a new governor are on the ballot today, and the next Congress will decide federal funding for Bay Area transit, health care and disaster aid.

Trump says U.S. military aid to Israel will end at the close of 2026

In a Fox & Friends interview on Election Day, Trump said aid he put at about $4 billion will stop at year’s end and that Israel can still buy its own weapons, adding that “they don’t need it.” He said he and Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu had discussed ending the aid for years and that Netanyahu raised the idea. The current agreement provides about $3.8 billion a year through 2028, and the timing and terms of any change have not been spelled out.

Why it matters: Military aid is set by Congress, so the makeup of the House and Senate chosen today will matter for whether and how any change happens.

Fox News, Nov 3, 2026

Science & tech

Voyager 1 reaches one light-day from Earth on Nov. 18

NASA says the 1977 probe will be about 16 billion miles away on Nov. 18, the distance light travels in a day, after 49 years, 2 months and 11 days of travel at nearly 38,000 mph. Its twin, Voyager 2, is expected to reach the mark in November 2035. Voyager 1 is headed toward the star Gliese 445, which it should pass at about 1.7 light-years in roughly 40,000 years.

NASA, updated Nov 2, 2026

Google dismantles an Android botnet of more than 1.3 million TV boxes

Google says it seized servers and domains tied to a botnet made up mostly of Android TV set-top boxes, and it is “highly confident” that maker Videostrong deliberately built in the backdoor and sold access to criminals. Researchers counted more than 1.4 million infected devices across 206 models between June and October, and Google expects the action to cut off at least 10 million more. Because the backdoor lives in the firmware, security researchers advise checking your box’s model against published lists and replacing it if it matches.

The Hacker News, Nov 2, 2026

Apple warns of “unprecedented” memory costs as inventory jumps 154%

Apple forecast a 45% to 46% gross margin for the holiday quarter, down from 47.9%, mostly because of memory chip prices that CEO John Ternus said will keep rising. Inventory reached $14.5 billion, up 154% from a year ago, and executives did not rule out more price increases on devices. iPad revenue of $6.2 billion missed analyst expectations, which Apple blamed on supply constraints.

TechCrunch, Nov 2, 2026

New York Times and three other papers sue Brave over AI scraping

The Times sued Brave Software in Manhattan federal court on Monday, and hours later the publishers of the New York Daily News, Chicago Tribune and Orlando Sentinel filed their own suit. They allege Brave’s 30-billion-page search index, sold through an API to AI companies including Anthropic, Perplexity and xAI, copies journalism without permission and sidesteps paywalls. The report did not include a response from Brave.
