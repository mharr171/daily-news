Issue No. 6

Thursday, October 29, 2026

The Federal Reserve held interest rates steady, but two officials wanted a hike. A federal judge refused to make California set aside 20,000 ballots, and the head of the state’s high-speed rail project resigned. San Mateo County voters weigh an $848 million college bond, Stephen Curry scored 44 in a Warriors win, and actor Patrick Stewart died at 86.

In this edition:

General news

Fed holds rates at 3.75% to 4% as two officials push for a hike

The Federal Reserve kept its benchmark rate unchanged after September’s increase, with Beth Hammack and Lisa Cook dissenting in favor of another quarter-point hike. The statement gave no clear signal about December, as inflation ran at 3.7% in September and energy prices stay high.

Why it matters: Steady but high rates keep mortgages, car loans, and credit cards expensive in one of the country’s priciest housing markets.

XTB, Oct 28, 2026

Judge refuses to make California set aside 20,000 ballots

US District Judge David O. Carter rejected the Trump administration’s request to set aside ballots from voters it says might not be citizens, citing a pattern of attempted election interference. Prosecutors conceded some people on the federal list are likely citizens, and the administration plans to appeal.

Why it matters: San Mateo County ballots are counted under the same state rules, so the ruling keeps local counting on its normal track.

CalMatters, Oct 28, 2026

High-speed rail chief resigns amid a travel spending scandal

Ian Choudri stepped down as CEO of the California High-Speed Rail Authority, effective immediately, after reports that nearly $600,000 went to questionable consultant trips. State Finance Director Joe Stephenshaw takes over a project now estimated at $126 billion to $231 billion.

Why it matters: The Peninsula is part of the planned San Francisco-to-Los Angeles route, sharing Caltrain’s corridor.

CalMatters, Oct 29, 2026

Measure V asks San Mateo County voters to extend an $848 million college bond

The bond would fund a new gym, arts building upgrades, and repairs at Cañada College, College of San Mateo, and Skyline College, extending an existing tax of $18 per $100,000 of assessed value through 2049. Critics point to a past bribery scandal tied to earlier bond contracts, which led to a former chancellor’s jail sentence.

Why it matters: A median-priced county home would pay about $386 a year, and Skyline and CSM are the closest campuses to Millbrae.

Palo Alto Daily Post, Oct 29, 2026

Sports

Curry scores 44 as the Warriors win at Minnesota

Warriors: beat the Timberwolves 119–114 on Wednesday behind 44 points from Stephen Curry; Jonathan Kuminga led Minnesota with 20. Golden State is 2–3 and hosts the Lakers on Friday. 49ers: bye week at 6–1. Earthquakes: waiting on the MLS Cup Playoffs.

Basketball Reference, Oct 28, 2026

Giants start a pivotal off-season with pitching at the top of the list

San Francisco finished with 24 players on the injured list and must trim to 40-man roster limits within days. The front office wants at least two starters behind Logan Webb and Landen Roupp plus a closer, and may try to trade a big contract, all before a possible lockout on Dec. 1.

NBC Sports Bay Area, Oct 29, 2026

Culture

Patrick Stewart, Star Trek’s Captain Picard, dies at 86

Stewart died Wednesday at his home in Los Angeles after a short struggle with Alzheimer’s disease and Lewy body dementia, his spokesperson said. He played Jean-Luc Picard across seven seasons, four films, and Star Trek: Picard, and Professor Charles Xavier in the X-Men films, alongside a celebrated Shakespearean stage career.

Space.com, Oct 29, 2026

Science & tech

Google unveils Gemini 4 Carbon, starting with cybersecurity defenders

Google calls Gemini 4 Carbon its strongest model for long, multi-step engineering work and cybersecurity tasks such as finding and patching vulnerabilities. Access starts with a group of cyber defenders, and Google says developers, businesses, and consumers will get it after more feedback and safety work.

Google, Oct 28, 2026

SpaceX test-fires Starship ahead of Flight 15

SpaceX completed static fires of Ship 42, including a full-duration burn of all six engines, after testing the Super Heavy booster that will fly with it. Elon Musk is targeting November for Flight 15 and wants to try catching both the ship and the booster, pending approval.
