Issue No. 9

Friday, October 9, 2026

Federal forecasters now say a record-strength El Niño is likely this winter, raising the odds of a stormy season on the Peninsula, as San Mateo County voters start working through four charter measures and a college bond. Gov. Newsom says he may commute every death sentence in California, and the unbeaten 49ers prepare for Seattle while Fleet Week’s Blue Angels weekend begins.

In this edition:

General news

Forecasters now see a record-strength El Niño as likely this winter

NOAA’s Climate Prediction Center put an 83% chance on the October–December period reaching historic El Niño strength in its monthly update on Thursday. Forecasters expect strong to very strong conditions to last through early 2027, and the World Meteorological Organization expects the warming to peak around December.

Why it matters: Strong El Niño winters have brought flooding and mudslides to the Peninsula before, so it’s a good time to clear drains and gutters and make a storm plan.

The Watchers, Oct 8, 2026

San Mateo County voters weigh four charter changes and a college bond

Countywide ballots for the Nov. 3 election include Measures L, AA, J and U, charter changes that range from making extreme-weather resilience a county priority to handing supervisorial redistricting to an independent commission. Voters will also decide Measure V, an $848 million bond for the San Mateo County Community College District, and mail ballots must be postmarked by Nov. 3 and arrive by Nov. 10.

Why it matters: All four charter measures and the college bond are on Millbrae ballots, so it’s worth reading up before you fill yours out.

Patch, Oct 8, 2026

Newsom weighs commuting every death sentence in California

Gov. Gavin Newsom said on his podcast that he is considering commuting the sentences of all 563 people on California’s death row before he leaves office in about three months, citing a recent botched execution in Tennessee. He already paused executions with a moratorium; a victims’ rights group called a blanket commutation an insult to the jurors who decided those cases.

Why it matters: It would settle, at least for now, a question California voters have repeatedly been asked to decide at the ballot box.

KTVU, Oct 8, 2026

Blue Angels and the Parade of Ships headline Fleet Week’s big weekend

Fleet Week’s busiest stretch starts today, with the Parade of Ships passing under the Golden Gate Bridge from 11 a.m. to noon and Blue Angels air shows from noon to 4 p.m. Friday through Sunday. Free ship tours continue at Piers 27 and 35, Fleet Fest runs at Fisherman’s Wharf over the weekend, and the event wraps up Monday.

Why it matters: Expect heavy crowds and traffic on San Francisco’s northern waterfront all weekend; organizers recommend transit, and robotaxis can’t drop off near the main viewing spots.

SFist, Oct 7, 2026

ICE agent shoots man during New York arrest as city leaders protest

An immigration officer shot and wounded a man during an enforcement operation in a New York City neighborhood near the Bronx on Thursday; a 5-year-old child in his car is believed to be unhurt. Homeland Security said agents were arresting a Dominican national with prior convictions and that the FBI is investigating, while Mayor Zohran Mamdani renewed his call to abolish ICE.

Why it matters: The shooting adds to a national fight over immigration enforcement tactics that has also played out in Bay Area communities.

Science & tech

Burlingame-built satellites set to launch a sharper alternative to GPS

Xona Space Systems will send six satellites up on a SpaceX launch later this month, including two built in Burlingame that will anchor its planned 258-satellite Pulsar constellation. The company promises centimeter-level positioning, compared with meter-level accuracy from GPS, and hopes to cover the U.S., Europe, Japan and South Korea by 2028.

TechCrunch, Oct 9, 2026

Google’s Gemini can now act as an agent for businesses

Google unveiled a unified Gemini agent that can plan and carry out multistep work, connecting to tools like Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack and company databases, and letting users choose other models such as Anthropic’s Claude. Each agent gets its own Workspace account and email address, with its actions logged under its own name; businesses get it first, and consumers later.
