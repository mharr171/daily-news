General news

Top story

Shooting at a Fairfield Spirit Halloween store leaves one dead and six wounded

Gunfire broke out around 8:30 p.m. Friday inside a Spirit Halloween store at Solano Town Center in Fairfield, killing one person and wounding six, three of them critically. Police said the shooting stemmed from a targeted incident involving one suspect, and no arrest had been made as of Saturday evening. The FBI is helping, and the mall canceled its Saturday trick-or-treat event.

Why it matters: Fairfield is about an hour’s drive from the Peninsula, and police are still searching for the shooter.

Developing

Tech firms, unions and the Warriors put $7.3 million behind the Bay Area transit tax

The campaign for the Regional Transit Measure, a 14-year sales tax of half a cent in four counties and a full cent in San Francisco, has raised $7.3 million from donors including Salesforce, SEIU, Meta, Genentech and Caltrain’s operator. The tax would raise about $1 billion a year for agencies such as BART and Caltrain, whose fare revenue has fallen sharply since 2019. Caltrain warns that without it, it could close 10 stations, end weekend service and eventually shut down.

Why it matters: Tuesday’s vote decides whether Millbrae keeps full Caltrain and BART service, and whether local shoppers pay a higher sales tax.

Developing

Analysis finds 42% of names on the DOJ’s “likely noncitizen” list aren’t on California’s voter rolls

A court filing by the advocacy group CHIRLA, based on work by UCLA Voting Rights Project data scientists, found that 8,880 of the roughly 20,930 people on the Trump administration’s list don’t appear in California’s current voter files. The analysis also found thousands of likely citizens wrongly flagged as ineligible. The filing feeds into the ongoing court fight over the administration’s push to set aside ballots.

Why it matters: The findings undercut the case for holding back ballots, including those of San Mateo County voters flagged in federal letters, as counting begins Tuesday.

Police union campaigns against Justice Kelli Evans in a retention vote

California’s largest law enforcement union is urging voters to reject state Supreme Court Justice Kelli Evans, a 2022 Newsom appointee seeking a 12-year term. Union leaders admit the effort likely won’t succeed and call it a warning about how judges apply new parole and sentencing laws. Legal scholars say the campaign threatens judicial independence; voters last removed justices in 1986.

Why it matters: Every California ballot, including those in San Mateo County, asks whether to keep Evans on the court.

Developing

NASA’s Swift observatory is days from an uncontrolled reentry

NASA’s Neil Gehrels Swift Observatory is expected to fall back to Earth in the next few days after a robotic rescue mission failed to lift it to a higher orbit. Most of it should burn up, and experts call the risk to people very small. Any surviving fragments would land within about 21 degrees of the equator, an area that is mostly ocean.
