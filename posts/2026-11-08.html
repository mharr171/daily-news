Top story

San Mateo County still has about 100,000 ballots to count, and the next update is due Monday afternoon

The county elections office’s latest report, from Friday afternoon, shows about 179,500 ballots counted and roughly 100,000 still waiting, nearly all of them mail ballots received by Election Day. About 850 ballots with signature or other problems can still be fixed until 5 p.m. on Nov. 25, and a hand count of 1% of ballots began Friday. The paper last reported Millbrae’s District 3 race with Gámez ahead by seven votes, so that race and others could still move.

Why it matters: The Millbrae council seats, Measure V and the regional transit tax are all still being finalized, and the next numbers come Monday by 4:30 p.m.

California’s Great America will close after a farewell season in 2027

Six Flags says the Santa Clara park, which opened in 1976, will stay open through Labor Day, Sept. 6, 2027, and then close because it will not renew its land lease, which ends in June 2028. The 2027 season starts in March, and no opening date has been announced.

Why it matters: The South Bay’s 50-year-old amusement park has been a day-trip staple for Peninsula families, and 2027 will be the last chance to visit.

CBS News Bay Area, Nov 6, 2026

Tesla plans to wind down Cybertruck production by mid-2027 to make room for its Cybercab robotaxi

Elon Musk said on X that Tesla will end Cybertruck output at its Texas plant next year. Sales fell to about 20,200 in 2025 from about 39,000 in 2024, far below Musk’s early forecast of 250,000 a year, and Tesla’s shares rose 4.7% on the day.

Why it matters: Tesla has deep Bay Area roots, and the move shows the company betting its future on robotaxis rather than a signature truck.

CBS News Bay Area, Nov 6, 2026

Good news

Good news

California’s Channel Islands foxes have rebounded from a handful of animals to thousands

In the late 1990s only 15 island foxes were left on Santa Rosa Island, after feral pigs drew in golden eagles that also hunted the foxes. Crews moved 44 golden eagles, removed 5,036 pigs and brought back bald eagles, and the article calls it the fastest recovery ever recorded for an endangered mammal, with about 3,086 foxes now on Santa Cruz Island and 322 on San Miguel Island. This Spanish-language piece, published Saturday, retells the recovery rather than announcing new numbers.

Xataka, Nov 8, 2026

U.S. politics & government

Top story

Trump names Stephen Miller his chief of staff as Susie Wiles steps aside

Trump announced Friday that deputy chief of staff Stephen Miller, an architect of his immigration policies, will replace Susie Wiles, who moves to his political operation, with Miller starting Monday. Wiles had been chief of staff for 21 months; a White House official called her departure amicable, while ABC cites sources saying Trump privately blamed her for the midterm losses. Vance and Hegseth praised the move, and Democratic senators and outgoing Republican Sen. Thom Tillis criticized it.

Why it matters: Miller helped design the immigration enforcement that has reached California communities, and he will now run the White House facing a Democratic Congress.

ABC7 News (ABC News), Nov 6, 2026; Fox News, Nov 6, 2026

Developing

Trump says he has offered the White House press secretary job to UFC chief Dana White

In a Truth Social post on Saturday, Trump said he offered the job to White, the head of the Ultimate Fighting Championship, and suggested UFC fighters could serve as assistants. White, speaking in London, said he will meet Trump on Monday and fly to Washington but did not say whether he would accept, and RTÉ notes it is unclear how serious the offer is. The job has been vacant since mid-October.

Why it matters: The White House has gone about three weeks without a press secretary, the official who briefs the public on federal actions that affect Californians.

RTÉ, Nov 7, 2026

Trump orders the Justice Department to investigate Democrats he accuses of threatening his team

In a Saturday post, Trump said he had told the Justice Department to look into Democrats he accused of threatening officials, citing impeachment talk aimed at Attorney General Todd Blanche, and of threatening Republican donors with prison. He called it extortion and singled out Sen. Elissa Slotkin; Al Jazeera notes he offered no evidence and Fox News notes he named no donors. Neither outlet reported a direct Democratic reply, though some House Democrats had earlier backed impeaching Blanche.

Why it matters: It signals how the next two years may play out, as the new Democratic majorities plan investigations of the administration.

Al Jazeera, Nov 7, 2026; Fox News, Nov 7, 2026

Three senior Justice Department officials, including the civil rights chief, are leaving

Attorney General Blanche announced that civil rights chief Harmeet Dhillon, national security chief John Eisenberg and the U.S. attorney for the Southern District of Florida are departing. The AFP report gives no reason; it cites Dhillon’s letter, as reported by U.S. media, saying she submitted it in October and agreed to stay through the election.

Why it matters: Dhillon pushed for states’ voter lists, a demand California fought in court, so her exit may change how the department handles election cases.

Agence France-Presse (via Arab News), Nov 7, 2026

Curry scores a season-high 41 as the Warriors beat Orlando in overtime for their seventh straight win

Stephen Curry scored 17 of his 41 points in the last seven minutes, including a key three-point play in overtime, and ESPN says it was his 16th 40-point game since turning 35. Rookie Yaxel Lendeborg added 23 as Golden State overcame an Orlando rally that forced overtime with two seconds left. The Warriors are the second team in NBA history to win seven straight after an 0–3 start, joining the 1950–51 Celtics.

ESPN, Nov 7, 2026

49ers will be without receivers Evans, Samuel and Kirk against the Raiders, but Bosa and McCaffrey are expected to play

Mike Evans, Deebo Samuel and Christian Kirk were ruled out for Sunday’s game, while pass rusher Nick Bosa and running back Christian McCaffrey practiced fully Friday with no injury designation. Receiver Demarcus Robinson and cornerback Nate Hobbs were questionable and need to be activated from injured reserve, with Kyle Shanahan saying Robinson was the likely one. The 49ers are 6–1 and coming off a bye; final inactive lists come about 90 minutes before the 1:25 p.m. kickoff.

Evaluator METR estimates Anthropic’s AI progress has roughly doubled in speed, with caveats

METR, an outside group that evaluates AI models, says progress on well-specified tasks at Anthropic is about twice its earlier rate, within a range of 1.3 to 3 times, and attributes most of the speedup to AI tools while stressing large uncertainty. It also says Anthropic redacted much of the evidence METR found most informative and cancelled a part of the engagement that would have verified how computing power was allocated.

METR, Nov 5, 2026

A lawsuit in San Francisco accuses OpenAI of negligence over an alleged ChatGPT-assisted poisoning plot

A North Carolina man sued OpenAI and CEO Sam Altman in San Francisco Superior Court, alleging his estranged wife used ChatGPT to plan to poison him in 2025, and he claims negligent design and failure to warn among nine counts. OpenAI says it banned the account and gave information to police; the claims are allegations that a court has not decided.
