Jobs & careers

American Express to cut about 3,500 jobs despite a 28% jump in profit

The card company will trim roughly 5% of its workforce, its first large round of cuts in about a decade, even after third-quarter profit rose to $3.71 billion. Leadership framed the move as an efficiency push rather than a response to weaker business, as rewards and perks get costlier to fund. The company did not say which teams or locations will be affected.

Why it matters: Profitable companies are still cutting corporate staff, so training, enablement, and onboarding candidates should expect tighter headcount and stress the measurable efficiency gains their programs deliver.

Watchmaking

Windup NYC fills a whole city block in its biggest year yet

Worn & Wound’s Windup watch fair spread across an entire New York block this year, its largest footprint so far, with more than 160 microbrands, independents, and bigger names on hand. Organizers expanded because demand from exhibitors and visitors had outgrown earlier venues. A brand-by-brand rundown of new releases from the show is still coming together.

Microbrand Watch World, Oct 22, 2026

How China’s watch industry grew into the backbone of the microbrand boom

A new long read traces Chinese watchmaking from its first wristwatch in 1955 through state factories, the quartz shake-out, and the Shenzhen supplier network that now makes cases, dials, and hands in small batches. It covers movements builders run into, including Seagull’s ST19 chronograph and the PT5000, a drop-in alternative sized like the ETA 2824 and Sellita SW200. It ends with buyer tips: confirm the exact movement, check service options outside China, and read owner reviews.

Tech & AI

Anthropic opens Claude Duet, an AI agent that works while your app is closed

Anthropic began inviting US Pro and Max subscribers from a waitlist to Claude Duet, a research preview that scans email and calendar each morning, checks connected accounts, and keeps working in its own cloud environment. It returns larger jobs as files such as briefs, decks, and dashboards, and pauses for approval before payments, signatures, or deletions. It shares the plan’s usage limits, and Team and Enterprise support is planned later.

VKTR, Oct 22, 2026

Meta pauses Muse chat sharing after thousands of shared links were found online

Researchers located more than 6,000 shared Meta Muse conversations because each link carried the chat’s title, and some held loan applications and internal business reports. Meta switched off sharing while it changes how links can be discovered. The practical lesson for any AI tool: share a summary or a PDF instead of a full chat, and review links you have already shared.

PCWorld, Oct 23, 2026

Board games

SPIEL Essen opens with tickets nearly gone for every day

The world’s biggest board game fair opened in Essen, Germany, on Thursday, using a new eighth hall for the first time. By Friday, organizers said Sunday tickets were also 90% sold, with Saturday and Friday already gone, and they warned there would be no ticket sales at the door. The four-day fair runs through Sunday, Oct 25.
