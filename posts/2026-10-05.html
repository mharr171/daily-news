Issue No. 5

Monday, October 5, 2026

San Mateo County opens its first vote centers today as Nov. 3 ballots arrive, and Bernie Sanders rallied in San Francisco for Prop. 40’s billionaire tax. The 49ers beat Denver 24–14 to reach 4–0, and the Supreme Court opens its term with a climate case that could affect suits filed by California counties.

In this edition:

General news

San Mateo County opens three vote centers as Nov. 3 ballots reach mailboxes

Ballots for the county’s more than 447,000 registered voters start arriving this week, and three vote centers open today in San Mateo, Redwood City and the South San Francisco Public Library, weekdays from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. through Nov. 2. The county also has 57 official drop boxes, and more vote centers open with weekend hours on Oct. 24. Mailed ballots must be postmarked by Nov. 3 and arrive by Nov. 10.

Why it matters: Millbrae voters can return ballots at any county drop box or vote early at the nearby South San Francisco library starting today.

San Mateo County Registration & Elections, Oct 5, 2026

Supreme Court opens its new term today with a major climate liability case

The justices first hear Suncor and ExxonMobil’s bid to end Boulder, Colorado’s lawsuit seeking damages for local climate costs; the companies argue climate change is a federal matter that states and cities can’t litigate. The term also includes challenges to AR-15-style rifle bans in December, a Colorado Catholic preschool case, and disputes over the administration’s deportation policies. Because many similar suits are pending nationwide, the Boulder ruling could reach well beyond Colorado.

Why it matters: Local governments in California, including San Mateo County, have filed similar climate suits against oil companies, so this ruling could decide whether those cases move forward.

NPR (via KUNC), Oct 4, 2026

Bernie Sanders rallies in San Francisco for Prop. 40’s one-time billionaire tax

Sen. Bernie Sanders, Rep. Ro Khanna and former Supervisor Jane Kim spoke to hundreds at the Curran Theatre on Saturday night in support of Prop. 40, a one-time 5% tax on California billionaires that would largely fund health care. Gov. Gavin Newsom and Mayor Daniel Lurie oppose it, and opponents have spent more than $80 million, over twice what supporters have raised. Two competing measures, Props. 41 and 42, would cancel it if they pass.

Why it matters: Prop. 40 and two rival measures that would cancel it are on the November ballot now arriving in San Mateo County mailboxes.

Sports

Weekend recap: 49ers move to 4–0 as Warriors open preseason in Honolulu

Sunday: 49ers 24, Broncos 14 at Levi’s Stadium, after a 3–3 halftime tie. Also Sunday: Clippers 104, Warriors 101 in the preseason opener in Honolulu. The Earthquakes were idle, and the Giants are not in the postseason. This week: the Warriors host the Lakers on Tuesday at Chase Center and visit Portland on Wednesday; on Sunday (Oct. 11) the 49ers play at Seattle and the Quakes visit Colorado.

San Francisco 49ers, Oct 5, 2026

Kittle’s 56-yard catch-and-run seals the 49ers’ fourth straight win

Brock Purdy went 19 of 30 for 218 yards and two touchdowns, and George Kittle’s 56-yard score with about two minutes left put Denver away. Christian McCaffrey ran for 52 yards and a touchdown as the 49ers piled up 140 rushing yards, while Dre Greenlaw had 12 tackles. Mike Evans played through fractured ribs, according to Kittle, as San Francisco heads to Seattle for its first road game since Melbourne.

Brewers walk off Padres closer Mason Miller to take a 2–0 NLDS lead

With two outs and the bases loaded in the ninth, Jackson Chourio lined a two-run single off Miller, who had walked three batters, to give Milwaukee a 4–3 win in Game 2. The Brewers lead the division series 2–0. Game 3 is Tuesday night at Petco Park in San Diego.

Science & tech

Space station changes command as Crew-13 settles in and Crew-12 packs for home

NASA astronaut Jessica Meir handed command of the International Space Station to Roscosmos cosmonaut Pyotr Dubrov in a ceremony on Sunday. Eleven people are aboard after Crew-13 arrived Oct. 1, with two SpaceX Dragon capsules docked as one crew unpacks and the other prepares to leave. Crew-12’s return to Earth is expected in the coming days.

NASA, Oct 5, 2026

Trump creates a ‘Super Intelligence Force’ to coordinate federal AI policy

President Trump announced a new team of senior officials, reporting to him and chief of staff Susie Wiles, to keep the U.S. ahead in what the administration now calls “Super Intelligence.” It will coordinate outreach to tech companies, infrastructure providers and community and religious groups. It follows a Sept. 29 White House summit where AI company leaders signed a voluntary accord; the administration has opposed formal guardrails on the technology.
