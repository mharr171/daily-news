Issue No. 13

Tuesday, October 13, 2026

A former 49ers executive and a contractor face charges in a $1.4 million kickback case tied to San Mateo County Event Center projects, and Peninsula sales taxes could top 10% if the regional transit tax passes. The 49ers are 5–0 but will be without suspended linebacker Dre Greenlaw next week, and the Warriors face the Lakers in Las Vegas tonight. Meta launched its first frontier AI model, and Marvel’s VisionQuest premieres on Disney+.

In this edition:

General news

Former 49ers executive and a contractor charged in $1.4 million Event Center kickback case

Prosecutors say a former longtime 49ers executive, hired in 2024 to oversee three county-funded construction projects at the San Mateo County Event Center, steered the work to a childhood friend who then kicked back part of the payments. A grand jury indicted both men on 20 counts of commercial burglary each, covering about $1.4 million; both pleaded not guilty and are being held on $750,000 bail. The case surfaced when a construction company told the county it hadn’t been paid.

Why it matters: Each Event Center project was paid for with $1 million in county money, so the case puts San Mateo County’s oversight of taxpayer-funded contracts under scrutiny.

Palo Alto Daily Post, Oct 12, 2026

Peninsula sales taxes could top 10% if the regional transit tax passes

The Nov. 3 regional transit measure would add a half-cent sales tax for 14 years in San Mateo, Santa Clara, Alameda and Contra Costa counties, and a full cent in San Francisco, to fund BART, Caltrain, Muni and other agencies. Stacked on local measures, rates in several Peninsula cities would pass 10%: Redwood City and Belmont would rise from 9.875% to 10.375%, and San Carlos could reach 10.375% if its own city tax also passes.

Why it matters: Every San Mateo County city, Millbrae included, would pay the extra half-cent on most purchases if the transit tax is approved.

Palo Alto Daily Post, Oct 12, 2026

BART will test its quake warning system during Thursday’s ShakeOut drill

At 10:15 a.m. Thursday, BART will trigger its ShakeAlert early-warning system as part of the International ShakeOut drill, so trains will slow to 27 mph and make a very brief stop. Crews will run simulated damage inspections at all 50 stations, and riders will hear a systemwide reminder to prepare for earthquakes. BART’s advice: sit down or hold on if a quake hits, stay back from the platform edge, and text family instead of calling.

Why it matters: Riders on the Millbrae and SFO lines should expect a brief slowdown mid-morning Thursday, and it’s a good prompt to review your own quake plan.

BART, Oct 12, 2026

PG&E restores power after a weekend of wildfire-safety shutoffs

PG&E cut power Friday in parts of Fresno, Kern, San Luis Obispo and other counties, restored it Saturday, then shut off roughly 11,000 customers in counties including Shasta, Tehama and Yolo on Sunday as fire risk rose. The utility issued weather all-clears by early Monday afternoon and said every affected customer had power back by 8 p.m. Monday. Alameda, Napa and Solano were in the initial scope, but San Mateo County was not.

Why it matters: San Mateo County wasn’t affected, but fall shutoffs can reach the Bay Area, so it’s worth checking your address on PG&E’s outage site and keeping backup power ready.

PG&E, Oct 12, 2026

Houthi missiles target Riyadh for a third straight day

The Saudi-led coalition said Monday it intercepted a ballistic missile fired at Riyadh and two more aimed at Khamis Mushait, a day after a strike on King Khalid International Airport killed one person and wounded 11. A Houthi attack on the airport Saturday killed at least 13 people and wounded more than 300; the airport will stay closed until at least this Saturday, and several airlines have canceled Riyadh flights.

Why it matters: The fighting is part of the wider regional conflict around the war with Iran, which keeps oil prices high and feeds the gas prices Bay Area drivers pay.

Al Jazeera, Oct 12, 2026

Sports

Today in sports: 49ers stay perfect; Warriors meet the Lakers in Las Vegas tonight

The 49ers held on for a 32–29 win at Seattle on Sunday to improve to 5–0, with Mike Evans catching seven passes for 113 yards and a touchdown and Renardo Green picking off two passes; they host Washington next Monday night. The Earthquakes drew 1–1 at Colorado on Saturday, stretching their unbeaten run to seven before Wednesday’s trip to Real Salt Lake. Tonight the Warriors play the Lakers in a preseason game at T-Mobile Arena at 7 p.m. Pacific on NBA TV, still without Jimmy Butler III and Moses Moody, who are out with knee injuries.

EssentiallySports, Oct 13, 2026

NFL suspends 49ers linebacker Dre Greenlaw one game for headbutting a Seahawk

Greenlaw was ejected in the second quarter in Seattle after a late hit on quarterback Sam Darnold sparked a sideline scuffle, during which he headbutted linebacker Ernest Jones IV, who wasn’t wearing a helmet. The league suspended him for next Monday night’s home game against Washington, pending an appeal set for Tuesday. Greenlaw, who rejoined San Francisco this season, has 37 tackles in five games, and coach Kyle Shanahan said he was disappointed by the decision.

News4JAX (AP), Oct 12, 2026

Culture

Jessica Chastain’s “Other Mommy” opens at No. 1 with $27.5 million

Universal’s horror film led the weekend with an estimated $27.5 million in North America and about $45.2 million worldwide, well ahead of its reported $18 million budget. The Social Reckoning opened in fifth place with $5.5 million domestically. Both numbers are studio estimates, with final figures due early this week.

FilmoFilia, Oct 11, 2026

Marvel’s “VisionQuest” premieres on Disney+ tonight

Paul Bettany returns as Vision in the eight-episode series, which debuts with two episodes at 6 p.m. Pacific on Tuesday. New episodes arrive in pairs on Tuesdays, and the episode titles, from “No Strings” to “Forever,” hint at a story about identity, corruption and starting over.

FilmoFilia, Oct 12, 2026

Science & tech

Meta releases Muse Wonder, its first frontier AI model

Meta’s Superintelligence Lab launched Muse Wonder and the more powerful Muse Wonder Full, which Meta says perform comparably to rivals’ top models and lead a benchmark for remembering and using what users tell them. Developers can use Muse Wonder through Meta’s API at $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output tokens, while Muse Wonder Full is limited to Muse Code subscribers. Both models will power Meta’s Muse agent.

TechCrunch, Oct 12, 2026

SpaceX set to launch a cargo Dragon with the station’s last new solar arrays

A Falcon 9 was scheduled to lift off from Cape Canaveral at 3:35 a.m. Pacific on Tuesday with about 6,300 pounds of supplies for the International Space Station on the CRS-35 mission. The cargo includes the final set of roll-out solar arrays for the station, plus experiments on artificial retinas, 3D heart tissue and brain organoids. The capsule is due to dock on its own Thursday morning.

Space.com, Oct 12, 2026
