In this edition:

General news

US pulls diplomats from Middle East posts after warning of Iranian plots

The State Department ordered non-emergency staff and all family members out of Saudi Arabia and quietly reduced staffing in Lebanon, the UAE, Qatar, and Jordan. An Oct. 23 intelligence memo said Iranian efforts to target US personnel have reached “advanced stages.” A union for foreign service officers called the moves chaotic.

Why it matters: Renewed conflict could push oil and gas prices higher again, and anyone with family or travel plans in the region should check State Department advisories.

CNN via KESQ, Oct 24, 2026

Seven more San Mateo County vote centers open ahead of Nov. 3

New vote centers opened Saturday in Burlingame, Daly City, East Palo Alto, Half Moon Bay, Pacifica, Redwood City, and San Carlos, joining three that have been open since early October. All 10 are open 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. daily through Nov. 2, and 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. on Election Day, Nov. 3.

Why it matters: Burlingame’s new center is the closest to Millbrae, which has no vote center of its own.

San Mateo County Elections, Oct 23, 2026

California voters face about $44 billion in bond measures

Three propositions add up to roughly $44 billion: Prop. 1 is an $11.25 billion affordable housing bond expected to create about 40,000 rental units, Prop. 38 funds immunology research, and Prop. 37 helps new-home buyers with down payments through private lenders. The state already spends about $6 billion a year paying off past bonds.

Why it matters: Housing bonds shape how much affordable housing gets built on the Peninsula, and all state bonds are repaid from the general fund over decades.

49ers activate Christian Kirk as Evans and Samuel sit out

San Francisco activated Christian Kirk off injured reserve, and he is set to make his 49ers debut today. With Mike Evans and Deebo Samuel ruled out, the team has just four receivers: Kirk, Brandin Cooks, Jordan Watkins, and Jacob Cowing. The 49ers also called up two practice-squad players for the game.

Jung Hoo Lee calls 2026 a ‘rollercoaster season’ as Giants weigh the outfield

Lee set career highs with a .276 average, 10 home runs, and 57 RBIs, but cooled sharply over his final 55 games after a strong first half. Manager Tony Vitello expects another step forward. With 10 outfielders on the depth chart, the Giants could trade from that surplus, though Lee’s contract and popularity make a deal less likely.

Clayface leads Friday’s box office as a Nathan Fielder documentary expands

DC’s Clayface took in about $10.4 million on Friday, including previews, and is on track for about $24 million this weekend. You Can See Everything, the documentary from Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim about Elizabeth Holmes in the month before her prison sentence, grew to 2,655 theaters and earned $6.1 million. The stop-motion film Wildwood, voiced by Carey Mulligan and Jacob Tremblay, was third.

Science & tech

Full moon, ‘Demon Star,’ and Halloween fireballs: what to see in the sky this week

The full moon on Oct. 26 is the best time to see the bright streaks around the Tycho and Copernicus craters, even with binoculars. Algol, the “Demon Star,” dims noticeably every few days, and slow, bright Taurid meteors can show up after dark, with the shower peaking in early November. The Pleiades rise in the east before midnight.

Space.com, Oct 24, 2026

Galaxy-sized black holes can’t explain dark matter, study finds

Physicists in Germany and Abu Dhabi used the leftover glow of the early universe to show that black holes bigger than whole galaxies can’t make up much of dark matter, since they would have left the glow looking grainy. The team is now testing whether much smaller, asteroid-mass black holes could fill the gap. The work is due in Physical Review Letters.
