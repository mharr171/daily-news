Issue No. 16

Friday, October 16, 2026

Mail-in ballots were stolen from a Daly City post office, and San Mateo County voters are urged to track theirs, while southbound 101 closes in San Francisco this weekend as the Valkyries host the WNBA Finals. BART announced a November bus bridge between Daly City and 24th Street that will slow Millbrae and SFO trips. Street Fighter leads a busy movie weekend, and a SpaceX cargo ship reached the space station.

In this edition:

General news

Ballots stolen from a Daly City post office; San Mateo County voters urged to check theirs

An unknown number of San Mateo County vote-by-mail ballots were in mail stolen from a Daly City post office, and the county elections office is investigating with the US Postal Service after learning of the theft Wednesday. Voters who are worried can request a replacement ballot online by Oct. 27 or in person at any vote center, and can track their ballot through the state’s free Where’s My Ballot service.

Why it matters: Any San Mateo County voter, including in Millbrae, can sign up for ballot tracking to confirm their ballot is mailed, received and counted.

Palo Alto Daily Post, Oct 16, 2026

Southbound 101 closes in San Francisco this weekend as the Valkyries host the WNBA Finals

Caltrans is closing southbound Highway 101 near Hospital Curve from 11 p.m. Friday to 6 a.m. Monday. The closure overlaps the Golden State Valkyries’ WNBA Finals home games against the Atlanta Dream at Chase Center, at 12:30 p.m. Saturday and 5 p.m. Monday, and Caltrain is urging fans to ride to Fourth and King, where Muni light rail is free with a game ticket.

Why it matters: Drivers heading home to the Peninsula from San Francisco should expect detours and delays all weekend; Caltrain from Millbrae avoids the closure.

Caltrain, Oct 15, 2026

BART will swap trains for buses between Daly City and 24th Street on Nov. 14–15

BART will halt trains between 24th St. Mission and Daly City the weekend of Nov. 14–15 to install equipment for its new train control system, with SamTrans express buses and Muni shuttles filling the gap. The Green Line will be canceled, Red Line trains will turn back at 24th St. Mission, trains between Daly City and Millbrae will run every 15 minutes, and riders south of 24th St. should plan on delays of up to 30 minutes.

Why it matters: Millbrae and SFO riders heading into San Francisco that weekend will have to switch to a bus at Daly City and should budget extra time.

BART, Oct 15, 2026

California’s next governor faces hard choices on Medi-Cal

Medi-Cal covers about 13.6 million Californians, roughly 1 in 3, and now costs $222 billion a year, more than 40% of state spending, just as federal cuts of more than $900 billion over a decade begin. Republican Steve Hilton wants to narrow eligibility and end coverage for immigrants without legal status, while Democrat Xavier Becerra pledges to shield residents from federal cuts but hasn’t said how the state would pay. An enrollment freeze for undocumented adults and new monthly premiums take effect next year.

Why it matters: About a third of Californians rely on Medi-Cal, so the governor’s race will shape coverage for many San Mateo County families.

KFF Health News, Oct 16, 2026

Teen gunman kills three children and a teacher at an Oregon elementary school

A 16-year-old shot his way through locked doors at Silver Rail Elementary School in Bend, Oregon, on Thursday, killing three children and a teacher before a civilian who followed him inside shot and killed him, police said. A 7-year-old and another teacher were critically wounded, and all Bend-La Pine schools are closed today.

Why it matters: The attack in central Oregon is likely to renew questions about school safety plans in districts across the West Coast, including the Bay Area.

Associated Press (The Atlanta Journal-Constitution), Oct 15, 2026

Sports

Today in sports: Quakes host league-leading Nashville Saturday; 49ers get ready for Washington

Fresh off Wednesday’s stoppage-time draw in Utah, the Earthquakes, unbeaten in eight and fourth in the West, host Nashville SC at PayPal Park on Saturday with a playoff berth in sight. The 5–0 49ers host Washington on Monday night without suspended linebacker Dre Greenlaw, the Warriors play their final preseason game before Wednesday’s opener at the Lakers, and the Bay Area’s Valkyries open the WNBA Finals at Chase Center on Saturday.

TSN (AP), Oct 15, 2026

Culture

New this weekend: “Street Fighter” in theaters, Chris Evans in Netflix’s “Sacrifice,” and a new Fontaines D.C. album

In theaters, Paramount’s Street Fighter opens after $4 million in Thursday previews, alongside Whalefall, A24’s Elizabeth Holmes documentary You Can See Everything and Sense and Sensibility. On streaming, Netflix has Sacrifice with Chris Evans and Anya Taylor-Joy, Prime Video has Stanley Tucci’s Mona Lisa heist Masterplan, Apple TV has the Everest drama Tenzing, and Tulsa King season 4 starts on Paramount+. New albums include Fontaines D.C.’s Dopamine Chamber, Babehoven’s I See Them, I See Me and The Avalanches’ No Bad Memories.

TVLine, Oct 16, 2026

Fontaines D.C. turn darker and more reflective on “Dopamine Chamber”

The Dublin band’s fifth album, the follow-up to Romance, takes a darker, more reflective look at modern life and tops Radio Milwaukee’s picks for the week. Other standouts include June McDoom’s harmony-rich folk record Follow the Light, Babehoven’s I See Them, I See Me and new releases from Rachel Chinouriri, Eels and HAAI.

Radio Milwaukee, Oct 16, 2026

Science & tech

Cargo Dragon docks at the space station with its last new solar arrays

SpaceX’s CRS-35 Dragon, which launched Tuesday from Cape Canaveral, docked with the International Space Station at 7:27 a.m. Pacific on Thursday after a roughly 51-hour chase. It delivered more than 6,300 pounds of food, research gear and hardware, including the last of eight upgraded solar panels, and will stay about a month before returning to Earth.

Space.com, Oct 15, 2026

NASA’s Roman Space Telescope makes TIME’s Best Inventions of 2026

TIME named the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope to its annual list of the year’s most groundbreaking inventions. Roman launched Aug. 30 and is partway through a roughly three-month trip to its final orbit while engineers test its instruments, with first images expected by early 2027. The telescope will survey huge areas of sky to study dark energy, dark matter and planets around other stars.

NASA, Oct 15, 2026

GitHub’s AI Scan for pull requests is now generally available

GitHub’s AI Scan automatically checks code changes in pull requests for security flaws in languages its CodeQL engine doesn’t yet cover, such as PHP, shell scripts, Terraform and Dockerfiles, and can suggest fixes. It’s available for public repositories and requires GitHub Code Security or Advanced Security for private ones; its findings are advisory and don’t block merges.

GitHub, Oct 15, 2026
