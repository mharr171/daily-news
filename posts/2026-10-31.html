Issue No. 31

Saturday, October 31, 2026

Light rain and drizzle could reach trick-or-treaters tonight as a coastal flood advisory continues, and San Francisco closes dozens of blocks for Halloween while the Valkyries host Game 7 of the WNBA Finals. Tech companies account for at least a fifth of outside spending in California legislative races. The Warriors beat the Lakers to reach .500, “The Odyssey” set Universal’s overseas record, and Saturn headlines the Halloween night sky.

In this edition:

General news

Drizzle possible for trick-or-treaters as the coastal flood advisory continues

New since Friday’s flood warning: forecasters now expect rain to spread north into the Bay Area later today, with light rain and drizzle possible during trick-or-treating and the best chances overnight. The coastal flood advisory runs through 6 p.m. today on the Pacific coast and through 6 p.m. Sunday around the Bay, with today’s high tide near 3:44 p.m. Daylight saving time ends at 2 a.m. Sunday, so clocks fall back an hour.

Why it matters: Families heading out tonight on the Peninsula should plan for damp sidewalks and highs around 60 near the coast and the low 60s by the Bay.

KION / KPIX, Oct 30, 2026

Halloween in San Francisco: street closures and a Game 7 crowd at Chase Center

Neighborhood trick-or-treat blocks across San Francisco close to cars from about 5 to 10 p.m. today, and 18th Street in the Castro closes from 11 a.m. today to 1 a.m. Sunday, rerouting the 33 bus. The Valkyries host Game 7 of the WNBA Finals at Chase Center at 5 p.m., with Muni running 78X shuttles from 16th Street BART. Sunday brings the Golden Gate Half Marathon closures from 5 a.m. to noon.

Why it matters: Peninsula residents heading into the city tonight, by BART or car, should expect Muni delays, closed blocks and heavy traffic around Mission Bay.

SFMTA, Oct 29, 2026

Tech money makes up at least a fifth of outside spending in California legislative races

A CalMatters review found Google, Meta and other tech interests supply at least 20% of the money behind outside groups in this year’s state Assembly and Senate races. Total spending on legislative races has passed $128 million, far above 2022 and 2024. Meta’s own committee spent $1.3 million backing a Senate candidate in a district near Silicon Valley.

Why it matters: Some of the heaviest tech spending targets Silicon Valley-area seats, so Peninsula voters are seeing much of this money in mailers and ads before Tuesday.

CalMatters, Oct 30, 2026

Iran’s first limpet-mine attack sets a tanker on fire near Hormuz

Iranian forces reportedly attached two limpet mines to a Panama-flagged tanker on Thursday, causing an engine-room fire in what maritime sources call Iran’s first successful attack of that kind in the conflict. U.S. naval escorts continue, and officials say about 95% of traffic through the Strait of Hormuz has passed safely. Houthi forces also claimed strikes on Saudi oil sites that analysts could not confirm.

Why it matters: Attacks on oil shipping keep pressure on crude prices, which feed into what Bay Area drivers pay at the pump.

Institute for the Study of War & Critical Threats Project, Oct 30, 2026

Sports

Today in sports: Warriors beat the Lakers to reach .500; Valkyries host Game 7 tonight

Stephen Curry scored 31 as the Warriors beat the Lakers 112–95 at Chase Center on Friday for their third straight win, evening their record at 3–3 despite 37 points from Luka Dončić. The Valkyries and Dream play Game 7 of the WNBA Finals tonight at 5 p.m. at Chase Center, and the Earthquakes host Real Salt Lake at 2 p.m. The Warriors host the Clippers Sunday, and the 49ers are on their bye.

Basketball Reference, Oct 30, 2026

Earthquakes close their home schedule against Real Salt Lake with home-field advantage in reach

The Quakes (13–9–10, 49 points) have clinched a playoff spot but sit fifth in the West and need to pass fourth-place Houston to host a first-round series. A win over ninth-place Real Salt Lake at PayPal Park today would put them in position to host Game 1. The regular season ends next weekend at Minnesota.

San Jose Earthquakes, Oct 30, 2026

Culture

“The Odyssey” sets Universal’s overseas record at $1.17 billion

Universal’s “The Odyssey” has passed $1.17 billion outside North America, the most ever for a Universal release internationally. The U.K. and Ireland, China and South Korea are its biggest markets, each above $100 million. The studio’s overall international take this year has reached $3.37 billion.

FilmoFilia, Oct 30, 2026

Paramount unveils “Ebenezer” posters with Johnny Depp as Scrooge

Paramount released six character posters for Ti West’s “Ebenezer,” a new take on “A Christmas Carol.” Johnny Depp plays Scrooge, Ian McKellen is Jacob Marley, and Andrea Riseborough and Tramell Tillman play two of the Christmas ghosts. The film opens in theaters Nov. 13.

FilmoFilia, Oct 30, 2026

Saturday TV: WNBA Finals Game 7 and Gracie Abrams on “SNL”

USA Network carries the winner-take-all Game 7 between the Dream and the Valkyries tonight. “Saturday Night Live” has host Inde Navarrette with musical guest Gracie Abrams, and PBS’s “Austin City Limits” features Ty Myers. Hallmark Channel and Great American Family both premiere new Christmas movies.

TVLine, Oct 31, 2026

Science & tech

Halloween night sky: Saturn and early Taurid fireballs

Around 7 p.m. tonight, Saturn shines low in the east-southeast, and a small telescope shows its nearly edge-on rings. Early Taurid meteors, known for occasional bright fireballs, may streak from the east-northeast; the shower peaks in the second week of November. Clouds and drizzle may get in the way in the Bay Area tonight.

Space.com, Oct 31, 2026

China’s Shenlong space plane makes visible predawn passes over the US

China’s reusable Shenlong spacecraft, on its fourth secretive mission, can be seen as a steadily moving point of light during predawn passes into early November. Los Angeles gets a high pass on Monday, Nov. 2, from about 5:42 to 5:51 a.m., and the Heavens-Above website lists times for other locations. China says only that it is testing cheaper round-trip space technology.

Space.com, Oct 30, 2026

OpenAI releases GPT-6 Rosalind for life sciences research

OpenAI released GPT-6 Rosalind, a model built for biological reasoning that can use scientific tools. Access is limited to organizations approved through its Rosalind Research trusted-access program. It follows this week’s GPT-6.1 Terra, a faster general model for writing and computer use.

OpenAI, Oct 30, 2026

October in review

General news: October’s election fights, fuel costs and storm prep

The Nov. 3 election shaped the month, as San Mateo County opened vote centers and voters weighed county charter changes, a college bond and a regional transit tax that BART and Caltrain say they need to avoid deep cuts. The Justice Department sued to set aside about 20,000 California mail ballots it called likely noncitizen votes, but a federal judge rejected the request late in the month. The Iran war kept pushing fuel prices up, with inflation reaching 3.7% and California gas topping $6 a gallon. With a strong El Niño expected, San Mateo County and San Francisco both declared local emergencies to prepare for winter storms.

Paramount closed its purchase of Warner Bros. Discovery, and Netflix announced about 1,000 job cuts as the streaming business reshuffled.
