Top story

The season’s first moderate storm arrives today, the first of three in a week

Steady rain reaches the Bay shoreline and the Peninsula between about 10 a.m. and 3 p.m., with gusts of 25 to 35 mph, brief gusts above 45 mph and a slight chance of thunderstorms; most of the Bay Area should get 0.3 to 0.8 inch by Saturday afternoon. A coastal flood advisory for the high tide runs until 4 p.m., and a high surf warning from 6 p.m. tonight to 4 a.m. Sunday calls for 25- to 35-foot breakers, with occasional 40- to 50-foot sets. A second storm is due Sunday into Monday and a possible third around Wednesday; forecasters don’t expect widespread flooding and say this is not an atmospheric river.

Why it matters: Expect a wet commute midday, possible ponding near the Bay shoreline at high tide, and dangerous conditions on Coastside beaches all weekend.

No BART trains between 24th St. Mission and Daly City this weekend; buses fill the gap

Free SamTrans express buses and Muni buses replace trains between 24th St. Mission and Daly City all day Saturday and Sunday while crews install a new train control system; Glen Park and Balboa Park get bus service only. Trains keep running every 15 minutes between Daly City, SFO and Millbrae, the Green Line is canceled, and departures across the system shift by up to three minutes. BART says riders going through the gap should plan for up to 30 extra minutes, and two Daly City parking lots are closed; regular service returns Monday at 5 a.m.

Why it matters: Anyone riding from Millbrae or SFO into San Francisco this weekend will need to switch to a bus at Daly City, so leave extra time for flights and events.

BART, Nov 3, 2026; Mission Local, Nov 10, 2026

Gámez’s lead in Millbrae’s District 3 race grows to 30 votes with few ballots left

In Thursday’s county report, Juan Gámez had 733 votes (51%) to Ann Schneider’s 703 for the short-term District 3 council seat, up from a lead of just a few votes earlier in the count. Albert Yam leads Bob Nguyen in District 4, 55% to 45%, and Sissy Riley is unopposed in District 2. The county says only about 1,700 ballots remain to be processed countywide, with updates due Friday and Monday afternoon, a Nov. 25 deadline for voters to fix challenged ballots, and certification on Dec. 3.

Why it matters: With so few ballots left, Millbrae’s council lineup for the next term is close to settled.

ICE arrests five people across San Mateo County in one morning

Immigration agents made two arrests in East Palo Alto, two in San Mateo and one in a San Carlos shopping center parking lot on Tuesday, according to the county’s Rapid Response Hotline and Office of Community Affairs. A faith-based advocacy group says at least 16 people have been detained in the county in the past 10 days, including arrests reported Monday in Redwood City, Half Moon Bay and Montara. U.S. politics & government

Top story

Trump threatens to double tariffs on Mexico over a migrant caravan, and Sheinbaum says it won’t reach the border

In a Thursday morning post, President Trump said tariffs on Mexico would be doubled immediately unless it stops a caravan of more than 1,000 migrants that set out this week from Tapachula, in southern Mexico; the tariffs in play include those on Mexican steel, aluminum and vehicles. Border czar Tom Homan said Mexico should send troops to its borders, and the Homeland Security secretary said the caravan would not be allowed in. Mexican President Claudia Sheinbaum said no caravan has reached the U.S. border since January 2025, that one group has already shrunk from about 600 people to under 100, and that Mexico will not use force.

Why it matters: Mexico is California’s biggest export market, and higher tariffs on cars and metals would raise costs for Bay Area buyers and businesses.

Mexico News Daily, Nov 12, 2026; KJZZ, Nov 12, 2026

Jensen Huang agrees to testify to three House committees, possibly by Thanksgiving

House Foreign Affairs Chairman Brian Mast said the former Nvidia CEO agreed to a closed-door appearance before his committee and the Intelligence and China committees, after lawmakers threatened a subpoena; Mast expects it to be recorded and transcribed. The committees want to know about Huang’s relationship with a woman U.S. intelligence suspects of spying for China. Rep. Darrell Issa said the deal was made while Huang was still CEO and may need to be reworked, while Democrats including Rep. Ted Lieu are pressing questions about selling advanced chips to China.

Why it matters: The hearing could shape export rules for Nvidia and other Santa Clara Valley chipmakers that employ thousands of Bay Area workers.

Starship Flight 15 is set for 2:45 p.m. today, pending FAA approval

SpaceX is targeting a 75-minute window that opens at 2:45 p.m. Pacific from Starbase, Texas, for the first try at catching both stages: the Super Heavy booster with the tower’s arms a few minutes after launch, and the Ship about a day later, if it can come down in the planned area. The Ship is set to make about 14 orbits over 22 hours and release 26 Starlink V3 satellites. As of Thursday, the flight still needed its regulatory sign-off.

ULA’s Vulcan returns to flight this morning with Amazon internet satellites

The rocket is set to lift off at 9:54 a.m. Pacific from Cape Canaveral on its first launch in nine months, carrying a batch of Amazon Leo broadband satellites, the first of 38 Amazon missions booked on Vulcan. Its last flight in February was marred when one of four solid boosters malfunctioned shortly after liftoff, and ULA says it has redesigned the booster nozzle. Space Force missions on Vulcan are on hold until the fix is proven.

Aviation Week, Nov 5, 2026

Google DeepMind releases AlphaGenome 2 to predict how DNA changes affect specific cells

The model reads a million letters of DNA along with a chosen cell type and predicts how genetic variants change gene activity, finding nearly twice as many expression-changing variants as the previous best model. On one benchmark it picked out the causal variant 30% of the time, versus 16% for earlier models. It’s available now to non-commercial researchers through the AlphaGenome API, with commercial access in private preview; it’s not for clinical or diagnostic use.
