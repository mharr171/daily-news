General news

San Mateo County urges early storm prep as a strong El Niño grows likely

NOAA puts the odds of a strong to very strong El Niño this winter above 83%, and county leaders worry most about storms that line up with high tides or hit already soaked hillsides. After declaring a local emergency, the county is clearing drains and culverts, mapping sandbag sites, and forming a task force that may include Caltrans and PG&E. Residents can sign up for emergency alerts through SMC Alert.

Why it matters: Millbrae’s hillside streets and bayfront flats face both landslide and flood risk, so now is the time to clear gutters, check insurance, and sign up for alerts.
