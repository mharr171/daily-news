Issue No. 9

Sunday, November 1, 2026

A shooting at a Fairfield Halloween store left one dead and six hurt, and a new analysis challenges the federal list of “likely noncitizen” voters in California ahead of Tuesday’s election. Clocks fall back an hour this weekend. The Warriors will rest Stephen Curry against the Clippers, and NASA is asking industry how to rescue the Hubble Space Telescope.

In this edition:

General news

Gunfire at a Fairfield Halloween store leaves one dead and six hurt

Police say a gunman opened fire inside a Spirit Halloween store at Solano Town Center on Friday around 8:30 p.m., killing one person and wounding six. At least three of the injured were in critical condition, and no arrest had been reported. Detectives are interviewing witnesses and asking anyone with information to call Fairfield police.

Why it matters: Fairfield is about an hour’s drive from the Peninsula, and the shooting happened during a holiday weekend when stores and events draw crowds across the Bay Area.

The Express Tribune, Oct 31, 2026

Analysis: 42% of names on DOJ’s “likely noncitizen” list aren’t on California’s voter rolls

Two UCLA data scientists reviewed the list the Trump administration sent California in early October and found that 8,880 of its roughly 21,000 names do not appear in the state’s current voter files. The analysis was filed in court Saturday by an immigrant-rights group and a candidate for governor, who argue that many eligible citizens were wrongly flagged. A federal appeals court ruled against the administration’s complaint a second time on Friday.

Why it matters: With Election Day on Tuesday, the fight over this list affects whether flagged voters in San Mateo County and across California have their ballots counted.

Al Jazeera, Oct 31, 2026

Daylight saving time ends: set clocks back an hour

Clocks “fall back” this weekend as daylight saving time ends, giving most people an extra hour of sleep. A sleep specialist interviewed by CBS News offers tips for easing the adjustment, which can leave some people groggy for a few days.

Why it matters: Phones update on their own, but stove, car and wall clocks do not, and darker evenings arrive just as commutes on Caltrain and BART shift back toward dusk.

CBS News, Oct 31, 2026

Sports

Today in sports: Warriors rest Curry, 49ers regroup on a bye, Quakes are playoff-bound

The Warriors beat the Lakers 112–95 on Friday, with Stephen Curry scoring 31, and play the Clippers on Sunday without him: the team says he is sitting out for injury management because of a lingering knee issue. The 49ers are on a bye after last week’s 29–20 loss in Atlanta. The San Jose Earthquakes have already clinched a spot in the MLS playoffs, which begin Nov. 18, and were fourth in the West when they clinched.

Yahoo Sports, Oct 31, 2026

NFL fines 49ers linebacker Dre Greenlaw for punching a Falcons receiver

The league fined Greenlaw more than $12,000 for a punch aimed at the ball that instead landed on Atlanta receiver Drake London’s head. Reports say it is his second fine of the season. The 49ers use their bye week to heal up before the Nov. 10 trade deadline that is fueling receiver rumors.

Science & tech

NASA asks industry how to lift Hubble into a safer orbit

NASA issued a request for information asking companies how they could raise, service or safely bring down the Hubble Space Telescope, aiming for a mission by 2032 that would lift it to at least 600 kilometers. Without help, Hubble is expected to fall back to Earth in the early-to-mid 2030s. NASA says it is only gathering ideas and has not committed to paying for a rescue.

Ars Technica (via Slashdot), Oct 31, 2026

Mistral publishes the weights of its trillion-parameter Large 4 model

The French AI company released the downloadable weights for Mistral Large 4, so companies and governments can run it on their own systems. It is pitched at cybersecurity work and coding agents, and Mistral says it matches a leading Chinese open model while using 47 percent fewer tokens per task. The weights are on Hugging Face and the model is also available through Mistral’s API.

TechCrunch, Oct 29, 2026

Data centers and AI become a midterm issue, even in Brooklyn

At a political event five days before Election Day, conversation kept returning to AI and data centers, according to TechCrunch. A recent industry report found nearly $200 billion of data center projects were delayed or blocked in the first half of 2026 amid local opposition.
