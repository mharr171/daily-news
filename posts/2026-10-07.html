Issue No. 7

Wednesday, October 7, 2026

The Iran war has pushed Muni’s fuel costs up 58%, and President Trump says he may seek a federal gas tax holiday as prices squeeze drivers. Fleet Week is underway with robotaxis barred from the waterfront this weekend, the Warriors routed the Lakers, and NASA’s Crew-12 left the space station this morning for a splashdown off California.

In this edition:

General news

Iran war pushes Muni’s fuel bill up 58% as transit tax votes near

SFMTA spent an average of $1.7 million a month on fuel from March through June, about 58% more than before the war, as the Strait of Hormuz blockade sent California diesel from $5.15 to $8.36 a gallon in a year. Muni’s 556 fuel-burning buses use about 300,000 gallons of diesel a month, and the agency already faces a $307 million operating deficit. SFMTA is counting on two November tax measures to avoid drastic service cuts.

Why it matters: The same fuel spike hits SamTrans and other Peninsula transit budgets, and Bay Area voters decide transit funding on Nov. 3.

San Francisco Standard, Oct 6, 2026

Fleet Week is underway; robotaxis barred from the waterfront Friday through Sunday

Fleet Week began Tuesday with at least 50 events over five days. From 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. Friday through Sunday, SFMTA will geofence much of the northern waterfront so driverless cars can’t pick up or drop off riders there, and they’ll be kept out of the Presidio entirely. Taxis, Uber and Lyft, private cars and transit are still allowed, and Muni is adding service to head off July 4–style gridlock.

Why it matters: Peninsula residents driving or taking BART and Caltrain to the air show should plan for crowds and expect robotaxis to be unavailable near the waterfront.

NBC Bay Area, Oct 6, 2026

Trump says he’s weighing a suspension of the federal gas tax

Asked Tuesday about waiving the 18.4-cent-a-gallon federal gas tax, President Trump said “We’re thinking about that,” a day after expanding access to tax-free diesel. The White House is worried about voter anger over fuel prices before the midterms, and a Reuters/Ipsos poll put his approval at a record-low 32%. Any suspension would need approval from Congress, where Republicans hold narrow majorities.

Why it matters: California drivers are paying well over $6 a gallon because of the war, so even a small federal break would show up at Peninsula pumps.

KTVU, Oct 7, 2026

Caltrain wins $14.1 million to make its 22nd Street station wheelchair accessible

A Federal Transit Administration grant will add ramps to both platforms at the stairs-only Dogpatch station, along with resurfacing, tactile tiles, lighting and signs. Design should finish by summer 2027, with construction done by the end of 2029 if funding holds. Muni separately won $2.4 million for four accessible boarding islands on the N-Judah.

Why it matters: Peninsula Caltrain riders with disabilities will gain a usable stop for the Dogpatch and Mission Bay area, one of the line’s fastest-growing stations.

The Voice of San Francisco, Oct 6, 2026

Sports

Today in sports: Warriors rout the Lakers 124–98; preseason game at Portland tonight

On Nellie Night honoring the late Don Nelson, rookie Yaxel Lendeborg had 17 points and nine rebounds and Stephen Curry scored 16 in 14 minutes as the Warriors beat the Lakers at Chase Center. Golden State plays at Portland tonight, where Damian Lillard could return from his Achilles tear. The 4–0 49ers visit Seattle and the Earthquakes visit Colorado on Sunday.

Most national power rankings put the 4–0 49ers at No. 1

After beating Denver, San Francisco tops most Week 5 rankings thanks to a high-scoring offense and one of the stingiest defenses, though Sporting News has it second and Sports Illustrated fifth. Injuries are the worry: Nick Bosa is out with a calf injury and receiver Ricky Pearsall is lost for the season. Sunday’s NFC West game at Seattle is the next test.

49ers Webzone, Oct 6, 2026

Jaylen Brown scores 22 in his 76ers debut in a rout of the Knicks

Brown scored all 22 in the first half as Philadelphia beat the defending champion Knicks 120–97 in the preseason, with LeBron James, Tyrese Maxey and Joel Embiid sitting out. LaMelo Ball also debuted for Minnesota in a win over Milwaukee. The regular season tips off Oct. 20.

Culture

‘Verity’ leads the box office as Tom Cruise’s ‘Digger’ stumbles

The Colleen Hoover thriller starring Anne Hathaway and Dakota Johnson opened to $32.6 million, ahead of Zach Cregger’s “Resident Evil” at $12.5 million and Brad Pitt’s “Heart of the Beast” at $11 million. Alejandro G. Iñárritu’s “Digger,” starring Cruise, took in just $8 million domestically against a reported budget of $125 million to $150 million.

Will Smith and DJ Jazzy Jeff’s origin story is headed to TV as ‘215’

Smith’s Westbrook Studios is developing a scripted series loosely based on how he and Jeffrey “DJ Jazzy Jeff” Townes came up in Philadelphia hip-hop. It’s the first project from a new partnership with The Malar Group. No network, cast or release date has been announced.

Science & tech

Crew-12 undocks from the space station for Thursday’s splashdown off California

Jessica Meir, Jack Hathaway, Sophie Adenot and Andrey Fedyaev pulled away from the station in their SpaceX Dragon at 8:05 a.m. EDT (5:05 a.m. Pacific) this morning after closing the hatch two hours earlier. Splashdown in the Pacific off California is planned for about 8:34 a.m. Pacific Thursday. Five other spacecraft remain docked, including the Crew-13 Dragon.

NASA, Oct 7, 2026

How to use AI visual search on your iPhone, Galaxy or Pixel

Apple’s Visual Intelligence in iOS 27 can now search what’s on your screen: take a screenshot with the side and volume-up buttons, then pick Search or Ask (iPhone 15 Pro or newer). On Galaxy phones, turn on Circle to Search under display settings and long-press the home button or bar; Pixel 6 and newer work the same way. Some apps block the feature, and AI answers are worth double-checking.
