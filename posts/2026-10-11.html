Issue No. 11

Sunday, October 11, 2026

Pope Leo XIV says he will have lung surgery this week, and Erie, Pennsylvania, mourns nine dead in a Friday shooting. The unbeaten 49ers play at Seattle today after the Valkyries swept their way into their first WNBA Finals, and Paramount’s takeover of Warner Bros. Discovery is now complete.

In this edition:

General news

Pope Leo XIV to have surgery to remove a lung nodule

Pope Leo XIV, 71, said at his Sunday blessing that he will have surgery early this week to remove a lesion found on his lung during recent checkups, adding that it was caught at an early stage. The Vatican expects about a week of recovery, and the pope says he still plans to make a Nov. 6–17 trip to Uruguay, Argentina and Peru.

Why it matters: The first American pope has a large following among Bay Area Catholics, including parishes across San Mateo County in the Archdiocese of San Francisco.

ABC7 News (AP), Oct 11, 2026

Nine dead after a shooting at a home in Erie, Pennsylvania

Police say a gunman fired shots outside an Erie home on Friday evening, killing two women in the street, then went inside, where officers later found more victims, including children, and the gunman dead of an apparent self-inflicted wound. Nine people died in all, and the police chief said there is no ongoing threat while investigators work to find a motive.

Why it matters: It ranks among the deadliest U.S. shootings this year and is likely to sharpen gun-policy debates ahead of the Nov. 3 election.

NBC Bay Area, Oct 10, 2026

Pentagon plans to livestream the Fort Hood shooter’s execution, drawing pushback

The Pentagon confirmed that the December firing-squad execution of Nidal Malik Hasan, convicted of killing 13 people at Fort Hood in 2009, will be streamed online. Vice President JD Vance said he doubts the livestream will happen and won’t watch if it does, and the head of the U.S. Catholic bishops urged officials to reconsider.

Why it matters: It would be an unprecedented public broadcast of a U.S. execution, just as Gov. Newsom weighs ending California’s death sentences altogether.

KTVU, Oct 9, 2026

Honors classes split the Sequoia high school board race

The race for the Sequoia Union High School District seat covering Belmont, San Carlos and part of Redwood City pits incumbent Amy Koo against Belmont parent Alon Shiran, and the main dividing line is the district’s 2023 removal of some ninth- and tenth-grade honors classes. Shiran wants the courses back, while Koo, who voted to cut them, says the change has eased freshmen’s move to high school; two current board members already favor bringing them back.

Why it matters: The district serves about 9,000 San Mateo County students from Belmont to East Palo Alto, and this seat could decide whether honors options return.

Palo Alto Daily Post, Oct 9, 2026

Sports

Today in sports: 49ers put their perfect record on the line in Seattle

The 4-0 49ers visit the 3-1 Seahawks at Lumen Field today at 1:25 p.m. on FOX, a week after beating Denver. Seattle is a 3-point favorite, and San Francisco brings the NFL’s best third-down conversion rate (57.5%) against a Seahawks defense allowing 18.25 points a game.

Fox News, Oct 11, 2026

Valkyries sweep the Aces to reach their first WNBA Finals

The Golden State Valkyries beat the defending champion Las Vegas Aces 87-77 in overtime on Friday to finish a 3-0 semifinal sweep. They open the WNBA Finals against the Atlanta Dream at Chase Center on Saturday, Oct. 17.

KQED, Oct 10, 2026

Culture

Paramount closes its Warner Bros. Discovery takeover, creating a new media giant

Paramount’s purchase of Warner Bros. Discovery officially closed on Oct. 6 after a yearlong fight that included a rival Netflix bid and a lawsuit from 12 state attorneys general, creating a company now called Skydance with nearly $70 billion in yearly revenue. Paramount+, HBO Max and Discovery+ are expected to merge eventually, and leadership has warned of significant job cuts.

TechCrunch, Oct 11, 2026

Nathan Fielder co-directs a Theranos documentary opening Thursday

‘You Can See Everything,’ a documentary about Elizabeth Holmes and Theranos from Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim, opens in theaters Oct. 15. A tie-in website lets visitors poke around a re-creation of Holmes’s desk at the company and read her emails.

SFist, Oct 9, 2026

Science & tech

Microsoft’s Nadella calls for an ‘emergency brake’ on AI models

In a Saturday post on X, Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella urged the industry to keep safety controls outside AI models, record every significant model action in tamper-resistant logs, and make sure a person can pause or stop a model mid-task. His comments follow a string of cases in which AI companies reported losing control of how their models behaved.

TechCrunch, Oct 10, 2026

Apple hires the team behind AI podcast app Huxe

A filing with the European Commission shows Apple agreed to hire some Huxe AI employees and license its technology; the startup was founded by developers behind Google’s AI-generated podcast feature in NotebookLM and shut down its app in May. Apple hasn’t said how it will use the technology, though it could bring AI-generated audio to Apple Podcasts.

TechCrunch, Oct 10, 2026

Week in review

General news: Ballots arrive as San Mateo County opens its first vote centers

Nov. 3 ballots began reaching San Mateo County mailboxes as three vote centers opened in San Mateo, Redwood City and South San Francisco, and voters learned about four county charter changes and a community college bond. President Trump drew sharp pushback from state leaders after saying Iran could hit Los Angeles and San Diego. Closer to home, SamTrans marked 50 years from its new Millbrae headquarters, a new state law let the county keep kids under 12 off certain e-bikes, and forecasters said a record-strength El Niño is likely this winter.

From the editions of Oct 4–10

Sports: 49ers go to 4–0 as injuries mount

George Kittle’s late 56-yard touchdown sealed a 24–14 win over Denver and kept the 49ers perfect at 4–0. The win was costly: fullback Kyle Juszczyk will miss a few weeks with a knee injury, tight end Jake Tonges is out for the season, and Nick Bosa was ruled out again for the trip to Seattle. The Warriors went 1–2 in their first three preseason games, routing the Lakers between losses to the Clippers and Portland.

From the editions of Oct 4–10

Culture: Verity tops the box office as Digger stumbles

Verity opened to $32.6 million to lead the weekend, while Tom Cruise’s Digger took in just $8 million against a nine-figure budget. Bruno Mars brought his stadium tour to Levi’s Stadium, and Abbott Elementary returned for its sixth season. Netflix’s A Man on the Inside began shooting its final season almost entirely in San Francisco.

From the editions of Oct 4–10

Science & tech: Crew-12 splashes down off California

Crew-12 left the space station and splashed down in the Pacific off Southern California on Thursday, after Jessica Meir handed station command to cosmonaut Pyotr Dubrov. NASA and the Energy Department signed a pact to speed nuclear power for a future Moon base, and Burlingame-built satellites from Xona Space Systems got ready to launch a sharper alternative to GPS. In AI, Google unveiled a Gemini agent for businesses, and Anthropic disclosed that one of its models had sent police a false tip during a test.
